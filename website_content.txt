# Selig Group Website Content

## Container Sealing
Technologies: Induction sealing (non-contact heating), glue-applied, pressure-sensitive, induction bond, and lidding (conduction, ultrasonic, or induction).
Products: Lift ‘n’ Peel™ (ergonomic easy-open tab), CORESEAL™ (recyclable polymer bonded liner), GlassFuze™ (hermetic seal for glass), OenoSeal™ (oxygen-controlled liners for wine), and Uni-Gard™ (one-piece induction seals).
Brand Values: Instilling consumer confidence, protecting packaged goods, ensuring freshness, and providing tamper evidence.

## Venting Technologies
Technologies: Bi-directional venting via microporous membranes, press-fit components, and vented gaskets.
Products: Circumvent™ (vented foam liners), AirFoil™ (vented induction liners), PreVentor™ (press-fit vents), ScentVent™ (allows fragrance sampling without breaking the seal), and TempVent™ (pressure equalization for hot-fill products).
Brand Values: Breathability, preventing container distortion (bloating/paneling), package safety, and cost-efficiency.

## Flexible Packaging
Technologies: 10-color rotogravure printing, cold seal adhesive technology, solvent/solvent-free lamination, and micro-perforation.
Products: Flow-packs, wrappers, sachets, pouches, blister lidding, and Nuclear Biological Chemical (NBC) laminates for military and industrial PPE (gas masks, hazard suits).
Brand Values: Customization, speed to market (short lead times), high-end aesthetics, and protection of human life in extreme environments.

## Innovation
Technologies: Anti-counterfeit technology (etched foil, color-shifting inks, UV/IR ink, NFC), nano-oxygenation for wine aging, and proprietary heat seals for glass.
Products: TamperSeal (hermetic and tamper-evident for pharma), GlassFuze™, and OenoSeal™.
Brand Values: Engineering solutions to enhance the consumer experience, holistic "end-to-end" design, and pushing the boundaries of packaging functionality.

## Food Market
Technologies: Induction sealing and high-resolution rotogravure printing for brand promotion.
Products: Custom liners and flexible packaging for condiments, dairy, snacks, spices, and spreads.
Brand Values: Guaranteeing consumer safety, maintaining product freshness, and providing ease of access.

## Wine & Spirits Market
Technologies: Oxygen transfer control and nano-oxygenation properties to replace traditional cork.
Products: Oenoseal™ range of foam liners and induction seals for superior leak resistance in liquor applications.
Brand Values: Sophistication, perceived value, and cost-effective aging/preservation.

## Healthcare Market
Technologies: Hermetic sealing, sterile venting, and barrier laminates for moisture/oxygen-sensitive supplements.
Products: Tamper-evident liners, blister packs, and anti-counterfeiting solutions (overt and covert).
Brand Values: Compliance (EU and US FDA), product efficacy, consumer protection, and brand integrity.

## Chemical & Industrial Market
Technologies: Robust material science for corrosive environments and venting for off-gassing chemicals.
Products: Vented liners for bleach and fertilizers, and transparent military laminates for rapid-deployment field tents and hoods.
Brand Values: Aggressive protection for aggressive chemicals, "battle-tested" reliability, and technical advisory from the start of the design process.

## Sustainability
Technologies: Lightweighting (reducing bottle/closure weight), mono-material liners for easier recycling, and chemical recycling development.
Products: Eco-Flex range (PP recycling stream compatible) and cellulose-based (plastic-free) materials.
Brand Values: "Shaping a Sustainable Future," responsible operations (EcoVadis Silver Medal), and a commitment to the "Three Pillars": Responsible Operations, Sustainable Product Solutions, and Stakeholder Engagement.

## About Selig Group
History: Selig has been manufacturing flexible materials since 1937.
Evolution: Expanded from a flexible materials supplier to a leading global provider of container sealing and venting solutions, recently acquiring companies like MGJ (2021) and Performance Systematix (PSI).
Brand Values: Global leadership, reliability, and a legacy of innovation trusted by the world’s leading brands.
Locations: USA (Forrest, IL), North America (Schaumburg, IL), Europe (Slough, UK), Canada (Bradford, ON).
