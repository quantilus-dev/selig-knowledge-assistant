# Sustainability Initiatives

Selig Group is committed to "Shaping a Sustainable Future" through innovation in material science and responsible operations.

## Three Pillars of Sustainability
1.  **Responsible Operations:** Focused on reducing the environmental footprint of manufacturing (EcoVadis Silver Medal recipient).
2.  **Sustainable Product Solutions:** Developing products that facilitate recycling and reduce material usage.
3.  **Stakeholder Engagement:** Collaborating with industry partners and customers on environmental goals.

## Technical Strategies

| Strategy | Implementation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Lightweighting** | Reducing the weight of bottles and closures to minimize material consumption and carbon footprint. |
| **Mono-material Design** | Creating liners and packaging using a single type of polymer (e.g., PP-based) to simplify the recycling stream. |
| **Plastic-Free Options** | Developing cellulose-based materials to replace plastics where feasible. |
| **Recycling Compatibility** | Developing the **Eco-Flex** range, which is fully compatible with PP recycling streams. |
| **Chemical Recycling** | Active participation in and development of chemical recycling technologies for complex laminates. |

## Notable Sustainable Products
*   **CORESEAL™:** Recyclable polymer-bonded liner.
*   **Eco-Flex Range:** Compatible with established PP recycling streams.
*   **Cellulose-based Materials:** For plastic-free sealing solutions.
