## Product Brands & Technical Specifications

### Induction Seals (Primary Sealing)
*   [Lift 'n' Peel™](Lift_n_Peel.md) - Premium one-piece top-tabbed induction seals (tamper-evident and clean peel).
*   [Top Tab™](Top_Tab.md) - Two-piece induction seals with integrated easy-open tabs.
*   [Safe-Gard™](Safe_Gard.md) - Two-piece heat induction foil innerseals providing robust weld seals (especially for HDPE).
*   [Uni-Gard™](Uni_Gard.md) - One-piece foam-backed induction seals (peelable and welded variants).
*   [DeltaSeal™](DeltaSeal.md) - Two-piece induction seals (foam or board backed) that separate upon opening.
*   [DuoSeal™](DuoSeal.md) - Unique one-piece double-sided induction seals for welding to both closure and container.

### Cap Lining & Wadding (Secondary/Compression Sealing)
*   [Uni-Foam™](Uni_Foam.md) - Co-extruded, pressure-sensitive, and faced foam liners for general cap lining.
*   [Tresylene™](Tresylene.md) - Extruded closed-cell EPE foam faced with polymer films (LDPE, HDPE, PET) for chemical resistance.
*   [AlphaSeal™](AlphaSeal.md) - Board or woodpulp-based liners laminated with barrier films (Foil, PET, PVDC).
*   [Corelen™](Corelen.md) - Foam liners faced with complex multi-layer laminates (including PTFE) for extreme barriers.
*   [Esterfoam™ / Esterfoil™](Esterfoam.md) - High chemical resistance retain gaskets with PE/PET coated aluminum foil.

### Specialty Markets
*   [OenoSeal™](OenoSeal.md) - Engineered foam liners with precise oxygen transfer rates, designed as a modern alternative to wine corks.
