# AlphaSeal™

## Purpose
AlphaSeal™ is a line of general-purpose wadding materials primarily based on a rigid board or woodpulp core. These liners are laminated with various barrier films and are commonly used in applications requiring a sturdy, non-induction, compression-based seal within the closure.

## Typical Composition
AlphaSeal™ structures are built upon a thick, absorbent, or rigid base layer, which is then laminated to functional barriers.
*   **Base:** Folding Box Board or Woodpulp (typically ranging from 0.80mm to 1.60mm in thickness).
*   *(Optional Binding Layer)*: Paper or adhesive layer.
*   **Barrier/Facing:** Aluminum Foil, PET (Polyethylene Terephthalate) film, or PVDC (Polyvinylidene chloride) coating.

## Key Variants

The variants are distinguished by their base material, the type of barrier applied, and whether the facing is single or double-sided.

*   **AlphaSeal™ Folding Box Board Aluminum Foil Faced PET One Side (e.g., 1.05mm):**
    *   *Features:* A robust 0.036" folding box board laminated with paper, 0.001" aluminum foil, and a 0.0005" PET film. Provides an excellent moisture and oxygen barrier.
*   **AlphaSeal™ Woodpulp PET Film Facing Both Sides (e.g., 1.30mm, 1.60mm):**
    *   *Features:* A thick woodpulp core encapsulated by PET film on both the top and bottom, offering chemical resistance and a clean appearance on both sides.
*   **AlphaSeal™ Woodpulp PVDC Coating (Single or Both Sides):**
    *   *Features:* Utilizes a PVDC coating (often 40UP) on the woodpulp to significantly enhance moisture and gas barrier properties without the need for a metal foil layer.
