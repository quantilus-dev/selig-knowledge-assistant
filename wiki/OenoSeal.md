# OenoSeal™

## Purpose
OenoSeal™ is a specialized range of foam liners and induction seals designed primarily for the Wine & Spirits market. It serves as a modern, high-performance alternative to traditional cork. Its primary function is to provide superior leak resistance and precisely control the oxygen transfer rate (OTR) to facilitate optimal wine aging.

## Typical Composition
The core of an OenoSeal™ liner is an extruded closed-cell foam produced from low-density polyethylene (EPE). This foam is then faced with specific high-barrier films on one or both sides.

*   **Core:** EPE Foam (e.g., density of 250 kg/m³, 2.0mm thickness)
*   **Facings:** Oxygen barrier films (e.g., "S Film", TIN, or PVDC) applied to the top and/or bottom to control gas exchange.

## Key Variants

The nomenclature often indicates the foam density, thickness, and the specific barrier film used.

*   **OenoSeal™ 2520 S:** 
    *   *Features:* EPE foam core (250 kg/m³ density, 2.0mm thickness) faced with "S Film" on both sides for controlled oxygen transmission.
*   **OenoSeal™ TIN PVDC Series:**
    *   *Features:* Utilizes TIN and PVDC (Polyvinylidene chloride) layers to create an exceptionally high barrier against oxygen and moisture, ideal for long-term preservation of premium spirits.
*   **Thickness Variations:** Available in various configurations (e.g., 3020, 3520, 4620) to ensure the correct compression and sealing force for different bottle neck profiles.
