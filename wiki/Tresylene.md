# Tresylene™

## Purpose
Tresylene™ is a range of high-quality foam liners based on extruded closed-cell low-density polyethylene (EPE) foam. These liners are faced on one or both sides with specific polymer films to provide excellent chemical resistance and low moisture transmission, primarily used as a secondary seal or a primary compression seal.

## Typical Composition
The structure relies on an EPE core laminated to functional outer skins.

*   **Top Facing:** Polymer Film (LDPE, HDPE, PET, etc.)
*   **Core:** EPE Foam (various thicknesses, typically around 1.20mm to 3.0mm)
*   **Bottom Facing:** Polymer Film (matching the top or different, depending on the variant)

## Key Variants

The Tresylene™ naming convention usually highlights the foam density/type and the facing material.

*   **Tresylene™ LDPE Series (e.g., 2512 LDPE, 25XX LDPE):**
    *   *Features:* EPE foam faced with LDPE (Low-Density Polyethylene) film. Provides a soft, compliant seal with good general-purpose chemical resistance.
*   **Tresylene™ HDPE Series (e.g., 20XX PEHD, 25XX HDPE):**
    *   *Features:* Faced with HDPE (High-Density Polyethylene) film. Offers enhanced rigidity and superior resistance to a broader range of chemicals and solvents compared to LDPE.
*   **Tresylene™ PET / ALU PET Series (e.g., 25XX PET, 3218 ALU PET):**
    *   *Features:* Faced with PET film or an Aluminum/PET laminate. These provide the highest level of barrier protection against oxygen and moisture transmission within the Tresylene range.
