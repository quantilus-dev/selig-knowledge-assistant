# Esterfoam™ / Esterfoil™

## Purpose
Esterfoam™ and Esterfoil™ denote a family of standard retain gasket liners designed to provide exceptionally low transmission rates and high chemical resistance. They act as robust compression seals within the closure, utilizing complex barrier laminates to protect the product.

## Typical Composition
The structure combines a thick, resilient backing material with a multi-layer high-barrier foil laminate.

*   **Backing:** Pulp (e.g., 0.020", 0.030", 0.035") or PE Foam (e.g., C25 Foam)
*   **Binding Layer:** Kraft Paper or specialized adhesive
*   **Barrier Laminate:** Typically includes multiple layers such as:
    *   Polyethylene (PE) Film
    *   Aluminum Foil
    *   Polyethylene Terephthalate (PET) Film

## Key Variants

*   **Esterfoil™ II (e.g., .020" Pulp Esterfoil™ II):**
    *   *Features:* Utilizes a pulp backing laminated to a clear PE/PET coated aluminum foil. Offers excellent resistance to chemicals and very low GTR Oxygen/MVTR. The contact layer is typically PET.
*   **Esterfoam™ / Esterfoil™ III (e.g., .030" C25 Foam Esterfoil™ III):**
    *   *Features:* Often utilizes a foam backing (like C25 PE foam) for improved compression recovery compared to pulp. 
*   **Thickness Options:** Available in various backing thicknesses (e.g., .015", .020", .030", .035") to accommodate different closure thread depths and torque requirements.
