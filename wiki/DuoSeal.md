# DuoSeal™

## Purpose
DuoSeal™ is a unique, one-piece, double-sided heat induction foil innerseal or gasket. It is designed to provide a welded seal to two different surfaces simultaneously during the induction process—typically welding to the closure (or a part within the closure) on one side, and to the container on the other side. 

## Typical Composition
The structure is symmetrical or near-symmetrical around a central foil core, without a separable backing.

*   **Top Sealing Layer:** Polymer Film (e.g., PE or PP Film, 0.030mm)
*   **Induction/Barrier Core:** Aluminum Foil (e.g., 0.007mm)
*   **Bottom Sealing Layer:** Polymer Film (e.g., PE or PP Film, 0.030mm)

## Key Variants

The variants define the polymer films used on either side of the foil, dictating which materials they can weld to.

*   **DuoSeal™ Standard PE/PE (or PE_PE 1002):**
    *   *Features:* PE film on both sides. Provides a welded seal to a PE closure on one side and a welded bond to a PE container on the other side.
*   **DuoSeal™ Standard PE/PP (or PE_PP 2001):**
    *   *Features:* PE film on one side and PP (Polypropylene) film on the other. Allows for welding dissimilar materials, such as a PP closure to a PE container (or vice versa).
