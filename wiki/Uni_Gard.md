# Uni-Gard™

## Purpose
Uni-Gard™ is a single-piece, foam-backed heat induction foil innerseal. It provides either a clean peelable seal or a strong tamper-evident weld seal, depending on the variant. The one-piece design means the entire structure remains on the container after opening (for peelable) or tears upon opening (for welded).

## Typical Composition
As a one-piece liner, the structure remains intact during induction sealing.
*   **Top Layer:** PET Film (often printable)
*   **Backing:** Polyolefin Foam (acts as a compressible layer to ensure even sealing pressure)
*   **Barrier:** Aluminum Foil (e.g., 0.001")
*   *(Optional Reinforcement)*: Additional PET Film layer.
*   **Sealing Layer:** Heat Seal (formulated for specific substrates and peel characteristics)

## Key Variants

The naming convention typically indicates the target container material and the foam thickness.

*   **Seal Type / Substrate Compatibility:**
    *   **ISCT:** Formulated to provide a **peelable seal** to PVC and PET containers.
    *   **ISPE:** Formulated to provide a **tamper-evident welded seal** to PE (Polyethylene) containers.
    *   **ISPP:** Formulated for PP (Polypropylene) containers.
    *   **ISM:** Formulated for multiple substrates.

*   **Foam Thickness (U5 vs. U10):**
    *   **U5 Variants (e.g., ISPE U5):** Utilize a thinner Polyolefin Foam layer (typically around 0.0045" to 0.005").
    *   **U10 Variants (e.g., ISCT U10):** Utilize a thicker Polyolefin Foam layer (typically around 0.009" to 0.010") for better compression and sealing on slightly uneven land areas.
