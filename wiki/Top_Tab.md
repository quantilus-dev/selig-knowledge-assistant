# Top Tab™

## Purpose
Top Tab™ is a two-piece heat induction foil innerseal that integrates an easy-open tab feature directly into the foil seal. After induction sealing, the backing separates and remains in the cap, while the foil seal—complete with its integrated tab—remains securely welded to the container, providing reliable tamper evidence and consumer convenience.

## Typical Composition
As a two-piece liner with a tab, the structure is relatively complex to allow for both separation and tab functionality.

*   **Backing:** Pulp (e.g., 0.020", 0.035")
*   **Release Mechanism:** Wax layer
*   **Tab Structure:** 
    *   PET Film (often acting as the top of the tab)
    *   Paper Tab (creating the non-sealing half-moon or pull area)
*   **Barrier:** Aluminum Foil (e.g., 0.001")
*   **Sealing Layer:** Heat Seal coating (formulated for specific containers)

## Key Variants

*   **Top Tab™ TT-529 Series (e.g., .020" Pulp TT-529):**
    *   *Features:* Standard pulp-backed formulation designed to provide a tamper-evident weld seal to Polyethylene (PE) containers.
*   **Top Tab™ 2 (TT2) Series (e.g., TT2-504, TT2-518, TT2-562):**
    *   *Features:* Second-generation tab designs offering improved peelability, wider container compatibility, and refined tab aesthetics.
*   **Thickness Options:** Available with different backing thicknesses (typically .020" and .035") to suit various closure designs.
