# DeltaSeal™

## Purpose
DeltaSeal™ is a line of two-piece induction seals designed to provide a reliable seal to a wide variety of containers, including glass, PE, PP, PET, and PVC. Upon opening, the backing material (either foam or board) separates from the foil seal, leaving the seal intact on the container lip while the backing remains in the cap.

## Typical Composition
As a two-piece system, DeltaSeal™ consists of a separable backing and a seal layer.

*   **Backing:** PE Foam (e.g., 1.8mm) or Board (e.g., 0.8mm - 0.9mm)
*   **Release Mechanism:** Often an interim layer like Polyolefin Film or wax that allows separation.
*   **Barrier:** Aluminum Foil (e.g., 0.0008" / 0.02mm)
*   **Sealing Layer:** Heat Seal coating (e.g., 25gsm weight)

## Key Variants

The primary distinction within the DeltaSeal™ line is the choice of backing material.

*   **DeltaSeal™ Foam Series (e.g., DS 2000 PE 2518):**
    *   *Features:* Utilizes a PE foam backing (e.g., 1.8mm). Offers excellent compression recovery and sealing on slightly irregular finishes.
*   **DeltaSeal™ Board Series (e.g., DSB 1000, DSB 1500):**
    *   *Features:* "DSB" stands for DeltaSeal Board. These utilize a rigid board backing (typically 0.8mm to 0.9mm). Ideal for applications requiring a firmer secondary seal or specific dispensing closures.
