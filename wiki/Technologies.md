# Sealing and Venting Technologies

Selig Group specializes in advanced container sealing and venting solutions, ensuring product integrity, safety, and brand value.

## Container Sealing Technologies

| Technology | Description | Key Product Lines |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Induction Sealing** | Non-contact heating of a metallic foil to bond a liner to a container's rim. | Lift ‘n’ Peel™, Uni-Gard™, Safe-Gard™ |
| **Glue-Applied Sealing** | Adhesion of liners to container rims using dedicated adhesives. | Various board-based liners |
| **Pressure-Sensitive** | Sealing activated by the pressure of the closure being applied. | Uni-Foam™ PS-113, PS-F1 |
| **Induction Bond** | Direct bonding of polymers using induction heat. | CORESEAL™ |
| **Lidding** | Specialized lidding films applied via conduction, ultrasonic, or induction. | Blister lidding, Flow-packs |
| **Hermetic Sealing** | Airtight sealing for sensitive products like glass containers. | GlassFuze™, TamperSeal™ |

## Venting Technologies

Venting solutions prevent container distortion (bloating or paneling) due to pressure changes or off-gassing.

*   **Bi-directional Venting:** Microporous membranes that allow gas flow in both directions while preventing liquid leakage.
*   **Press-fit Components:** Mechanical vents integrated into closures.
*   **Vented Gaskets:** Gaskets designed with channels for gas exchange.
*   **Nano-Oxygenation:** Precise control of oxygen transfer rates (OTR), primarily for wine aging.

### Key Venting Products:
*   **Circumvent™:** Vented foam liners.
*   **AirFoil™:** Vented induction liners.
*   **PreVentor™:** Press-fit vents.
*   **ScentVent™:** Fragrance-sampling seal.
*   **TempVent™:** Pressure equalization for hot-fill applications.
