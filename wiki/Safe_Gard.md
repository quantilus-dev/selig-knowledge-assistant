# Safe-Gard™

## Purpose
Safe-Gard™ is a two-piece, heat induction foil innerseal designed to provide a secure weld seal to plastic containers (typically HDPE). It acts as a robust barrier protecting product integrity and preventing leaks, particularly suited for demanding environments.

## Typical Composition
As a two-piece liner, the structure typically includes a backing material that separates from the seal after induction heating.
*   **Backing:** Pulp (e.g., 0.036" / 0.90mm)
*   **Release Layer:** Wax
*   **Barrier:** Aluminum Foil (e.g., 0.001" to 0.0012")
*   **Reinforcement:** PET Film
*   **Sealing Layer:** Heat Seal (formulated for specific substrates like HDPE)

## Key Variants

*   **1.0mm Safe-Gard plus™ 735:**
    *   *Features:* Provides a standard weld seal to HDPE containers.
    *   *Barrier:* High barrier with essentially zero GTR Oxygen and MVTR.
*   **1.0mm Safe-Gard plus™ 730:**
    *   *Features:* Specialized formulation intended exclusively for use with **agrochemical fillings**, providing a durable weld seal to HDPE in chemically aggressive applications.
*   **Safe-Gard plus™ (various thicknesses):**
    *   Available in different thicknesses (e.g., 0.6mm, 0.83mm, 1.0mm) to accommodate different closure and container thread designs, ensuring optimal pressure during induction sealing.
