# Lift 'n' Peel™

## Purpose
Lift 'n' Peel™ is a premium, one-piece top-tabbed induction seal featuring an integrated ergonomic half-moon tab for easy opening. It is designed to provide excellent tamper evidence, protect product freshness, and enhance the consumer experience with an easy, reliable peel. Suitable for sealing to PE, PP, PVC, PET, PS, and glass containers.

## Typical Composition
While specific layers vary by product, a typical Lift 'n' Peel™ structure includes:
*   **Top Layer:** PET Film (often custom printed)
*   **Tab:** PET Tab (integrated half-moon)
*   **Backing:** Polyolefin Foam
*   **Barrier:** Aluminum Foil
*   **Sealing Layer:** Heat Seal (formulated for specific container substrates)
*   *(Optional)* **Paper Layer:** White paper for specific tamper-evident tearing mechanisms.

## Key Variants

*   **Lift 'n' Peel™ MAX U5 LP-H:** 
    *   *Features:* Tamper-evident design. Upon removal, paper remains on the container finish as visual tamper evidence.
    *   *Applications:* Suggested for dry products only (liquid not recommended). Venting of oxygen and water vapor occurs through the paper layer.
*   **Lift 'n' Peel™ LPT (European):**
    *   *Features:* Clean peel design. High barrier (GTR Oxygen and MVTR are essentially zero).
    *   *Applications:* Designed specifically for use on PVC and PET containers.
*   **Lift 'n' Peel™ LPF / LPTZD / LP-M / LP-T:** 
    *   *Features:* Variations tailored for different peeling experiences (clean peel vs. tamper evident) and specific container materials.
