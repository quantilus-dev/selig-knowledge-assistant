# Uni-Foam™

## Purpose
Uni-Foam™ encompasses a broad range of foam-based liners designed for cap lining. They can act as primary seals (like pressure-sensitive variants) or as resilient backing materials that provide reliable compression and secondary sealing after an primary induction seal is removed. 

## Typical Composition
The composition varies significantly based on the specific product type:

*   **Co-extruded Foam (e.g., LD-19):** A three-ply structure consisting of a closed-cell Low Density Polyethylene (LDPE) foam core sandwiched between two layers of solid LDPE skin.
*   **Pressure Sensitive (e.g., PS-113):** A single layer of uniform polystyrene foam coated on one side with a specially formulated torque-activated adhesive.
*   **Faced Foam (e.g., EPE 30/42):** Expanded Polyethylene (EPE) foam laminated with various barrier films (PET, PVDC, Aluminum Foil) on one or both sides to enhance chemical resistance and reduce vapor transmission.

## Key Variants

*   **Uni-Foam US™ LD-19:**
    *   *Features:* Three-ply co-extruded LDPE foam. Density is typically 19 lbs/cubic foot.
    *   *Thicknesses:* Commonly available in .030", .035", and .040".
*   **Uni-Foam™ PS-113 / PS-F1 (Pressure Sensitive):**
    *   *Features:* Provides a seal activated by the torque of applying the closure. 
    *   *Limitations:* Generally not approved for use with wet products.
*   **Uni-Foam™ EPE 30 / EPE 42:**
    *   *Features:* Expanded Polyethylene base, often faced with specific barriers for enhanced performance.
    *   *Examples:* "EPE 30 Faced Saranex Both Sides", "EPE 42 Faced with Aluminum Foil & PET Film".
