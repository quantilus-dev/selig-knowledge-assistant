# Corelen™

## Purpose
Corelen™ is a specialized series of foam liners designed for demanding applications requiring robust barriers. These liners are constructed from extruded closed-cell low-density polyethylene (EPE) foam faced with multi-layer complex laminates, offering exceptional resistance to chemical attack, moisture, and gas permeation.

## Typical Composition
The structure typically consists of a compliant EPE foam backing laminated to a highly engineered barrier facing.

*   **Backing:** PE Foam (e.g., 1.5mm, density of 250 kg/m³)
*   *(Binding Layer)*: Polymer Assembly
*   **Complex Facing:** A multi-layer laminate that might include:
    *   White Paper (for stiffness/bonding)
    *   PE or PET films
    *   Aluminum Foil
    *   Specialty films (like PTFE)

## Key Variants

The variants are distinguished by the specific composition of their complex facings.

*   **Corelen™ PE Faced (e.g., Kb-PE-Alu-PE):**
    *   *Features:* The facing includes Kraft paper (Kb), Polyethylene, Aluminum Foil, and a final PE sealing/contact layer. Provides an excellent moisture and oxygen barrier.
*   **Corelen™ PE Faced (e.g., Kb-Alu-PET):**
    *   *Features:* Utilizes Aluminum Foil and PET film in the facing for high barrier performance and chemical resistance.
*   **Corelen™ PTFE:**
    *   *Features:* Faced with Polytetrafluoroethylene (PTFE/Teflon). This provides the ultimate in chemical resistance, suitable for highly aggressive or corrosive chemicals.
